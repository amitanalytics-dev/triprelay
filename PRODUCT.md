# TripRelay product

## Job
When a client requests or changes a Vietnam holiday, a small travel agent wants a complete suitable branded quote quickly enough to keep that client's interest.

## Person and alternative
The agency owns its client relationship and currently gathers/prices supplier details manually. V1 serves the agency preparing an enquiry; it is free.

## Core action
Provide and confirm a client enquiry, retain all requested date/hotel/transport choices, receive a complete supplier-priced quote after human review and download the agency-branded PDF.

## Boundaries
Unknown dates, nationality, rooms/travellers, routing, taxes, fees or availability block pricing. Supplier identities/costs stay private. No payments, booking, tickets or visas. WhatsApp delivery is postponed.

## Flow
Sign in; save agency details/logo; save/confirm the brief; answer missing questions; prepare supplier-backed alternatives; verify complete facts; review the exact quote; deliver the approved branded output. Corrections invalidate older source approval and stale delivery.

## Proof
A real enquiry reaches a fresh priced, reviewed final quote and an independent user walks the flow. Samples, an accessible preview and coding helpers do not prove this.

## Current limits
The foundation and guarded quote/review/PDF implementation are checked. Actual supplier access, complete verified sources, exact reviewer identity and genuine use remain unfinished. Main Flow is not claimed.
